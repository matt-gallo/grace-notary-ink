What actually happens at a loan signing? 🏡

The first time I set a loan package on someone's kitchen table, they asked if I'd brought the wrong file. 140 pages. One house. Nobody warns you about the size of it — so here's the short version:

⏱ Plan on about an hour. What stretches it isn't the page count, it's a missing ID or a name that doesn't match your documents.

✍️ The Note gets signed. The Deed of Trust gets notarized — it's the longest thing in the stack.

📞 Keep your loan officer's number close. If a number on the page doesn't match what you were told, we pause and you call. That's the right move every time.

🚫 Sign nothing ahead of time. Not one page.

I can tell you what each document is called and where to sign. What the terms mean is a question for your loan officer, title officer, or an attorney — not your notary.
